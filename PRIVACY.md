# Privacy Policy — Clinic Lead Desk (Development Test System)

**Last updated: 5 September 2026**

This is a development and testing application operated by an individual
developer. It is not a commercial service, and it does not serve real
patients or customers.

## What this application does

It receives WhatsApp messages sent to a Meta-provided WhatsApp Cloud API
**test** phone number and replies with fixed, pre-written administrative
messages. Every reply is automated and selected from a fixed list. The
system provides no medical advice, makes no clinical judgements, and does
not confirm appointments.

## What information is collected

Only the following:

- The sender's WhatsApp phone number
- The WhatsApp display name, if provided by WhatsApp
- The text of messages sent to the test number
- Timestamps of those messages

It does **not** collect or store health records, medical history,
photographs, medical reports, prescriptions, identity documents, or payment
information.

## Why it is collected

Solely to test whether the software correctly receives, categorises,
records, and replies to messages.

## Where it is stored

In a private PostgreSQL database. This information is not shared with, sold
to, or disclosed to any third party.

## Who can message this number

Only phone numbers explicitly added as approved test recipients in Meta's
developer console. This number cannot be messaged by the general public.

## Clinic details are fictional

Any clinic name, address, opening hours, prices, or services appearing in
automated replies are invented test data for a demo clinic that does not
exist. They describe no real business, and no real business is represented.

## Retention and deletion

Test data is deleted on request, and when testing concludes. To request
deletion of your data, or for any question about this policy, contact:

**aneesfreddy619@gmail.com**

## How to opt out

Send the message `STOP` to the test number. Automated replies stop
immediately and permanently.
