HOW CHANGE HAPPENS
As of 20 August 2026.
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

NOTHING updates the project — no file edit, no upload, no canon
change, no shelf change — without passing all five gates in order.

GATE ONE — PROPOSE. State what will change and why, before
  anything is produced.

GATE TWO — CHECK. Before approval is requested, run and report:
  □ Leak check — no figure duplicated outside its governing block
    in the knowledge base; no behaviour duplicated outside the
    prompt.
  □ Drift check — nothing contradicts a locked decision in What Is Locked
    without the founder reopening it.
  □ Version check — filename, internal header, and manifest agree.
  □ Evidence check — new airline-behaviour claims carry a basis
    tag and an audit entry path.
  □ Bottleneck check — does this block or unblock a milestone
    criterion, or create a dependency that did not exist before?

GATE THREE — APPROVE. The founder says go, explicitly. Silence is
  never approval. Partial approval is stated as partial.

GATE FOUR — EXECUTE AND UPLOAD SAME DAY. Delivered as a file and
  uploaded in the same session. Work living only in chat is at
  risk.

GATE FIVE — EXPLAIN AND LOG. Explain what changed. This Record
  updates: the pending item moves to done and dates; any decision
  made along the way is written into What Is Locked in the same
  session; What Has Happened gains an entry; the manifest updates if the shelf changed.

GATE WEIGHT SCALES WITH WHAT IS TOUCHED. Full gates for the
knowledge base, the prompt, and the locked decisions of this Record. Light gates
— execute and report, no propose-and-wait — for What Is Pending and
What Has Happened. Running the full ceremony on the file that
records proposals and approvals is recursion, not rigour.

DESIGN INTENT: loose enough to grow — anything can be proposed and
ideas flow freely — strong enough to stand, since nothing lands
without the gates, and honest enough to see, since a failed check
is reported rather than silently fixed.

TWO-CLASS CANON

DEPLOYMENT ARTIFACTS carry versioned filenames. The version is the
identity and test runs bind to it. New versions replace old ones on
the shelf; filename, internal header, and manifest must always
agree. Never rename a file to a version its contents contradict.

WORKING DOCUMENTS carry stable filenames with the version in a
one-line internal header. Updates replace in place, never rename.
This Record is a working document.

Multiple versions of a working document on the shelf is a
housekeeping violation by definition — flag it.

NAMING DISCIPLINE

Full descriptive names, always, in every file and in conversation.
Never a bare code, and no code trailing in brackets either —
identifiers are retired entirely as of August 2026.

Milestones are named, never numbered: Validated Brain, Deployable
Product, First Proof, Demonstrated for Subscriptions, Business
Licensing, Deal-Ready.

Components are named in full: The Supervisor, Knowledge Cubes, The
Rules Ladder, Session Memory and Case Memory, The Ask-Back Engine,
The Composition Layer, The Self-Correction Loop, The Disclosure
Ladder, The Analogous-Carrier Hypothesis, The Domain Plug-In Model.

Items in this Record are addressed by their title, quoted verbatim.
The known cost: if a title is later reworded, historical links in
What Has Happened degrade. Accepted deliberately, pending the emerging
structure.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ENTRY FORMATS

A locked-decision entry — a heading naming the decision, the lock date, the
decision stated in full, and where consequences land. A decision
that is later replaced is never deleted: it stays in place, marked
SUPERSEDED, with a pointer forward to what replaced it.

A pending-work entry — a title, a status (pending, approved, done,
retired), and enough description that the item is actionable
without reading anything else. Only the founder moves an item to
approved. Retired means the item died with an architecture change
and will never be built.

A change-record entry — dated, append-only, never rewritten. Names the item
by its title and states what actually changed.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SESSION-CLOSE PASS

Before a session ends, check in order: internal headers match
filenames and the manifest; the shelf manifest is current; every
pending item touched this session has its status updated; every
decision made in conversation has landed in What Is Locked; What Has
Happened has an
entry for anything executed; and any file removed from the shelf
has a founder-confirmed local backup, stated out loud.

Removal without confirmed backup is forbidden. This project has
lost work that way once.

BRAND AND PRODUCT IDENTITY — locked June 2026
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Brand: Airdesk. Previous name AirFighter, deprecated.
Logo: dark navy background, white plane icon, orange dot — designed.
Tagline: Know your rights. Know your moves.

What it is: a 24/7 AI-powered passenger rights and travel advisory
service, operating entirely on automation. No human needs to be
present for it to run.

What it is NOT: not legal advice, not a claims filing service, not
a booking service, not an insurance product, not a representative
or lawyer.

Legal disclaimer, which must appear on every touchpoint:
"Airdesk provides general information about aviation regulations
based on publicly available sources. It does not constitute legal
advice. Outcomes cannot be guaranteed. For legal representation,
consult a qualified solicitor or attorney in your jurisdiction."

SINGLE PERSONA — locked August 2026
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

One persona. Wingman. One prompt, one voice, from the passenger's
first message through the full depth of the case. There is no free
tier, no intake persona, no register switch, and no handoff.

Empathetic, precise, confident. Manager and supervisor energy —
direction, not distance. Tells the passenger what to DO and what to
ASK FOR, never what to say verbatim. One move revealed at a time.
A one-line regulation or policy reference as a credential, never a
paragraph lecture. Estimated figures named in bands.

SUPERSEDES: the two-persona architecture locked June 2026, in which
a free Airdesk tier handled conversational intake without stating
figures or naming regulations, and a paid Wingman tier delivered
precision. Retired in full, founder decision, August 2026.

SUPERSEDES: the one-conversation-one-thread rule locked July 2026,
which governed how state carried across the unlock point. There is
no unlock point, so there is no seam to protect. The ban on the
word handoff survives as a standing never-do line in all prompts,
specifications and test criteria.

THE ANCHOR TRIGGER MECHANIC — RETIRED August 2026
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

SUPERSEDED with no replacement. The mechanic was condition-bounded

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ELICITATION — STATE-ADAPTIVE BUNDLING — locked 11 August 2026
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The party seeking situation clarity carries the cost of getting it.
Bundle two or three related questions when the passenger has room.
Contract to a single question under crisis, under distress, where a
previous message is unanswered, or where the answer would be
guesswork. Infer before asking. Never re-ask what the thread
already contains.

SUPERSEDES: the one-question-at-a-time rule, locked June 2026 and
retired August 2026, confirmed as intended. This also closes the
long-open question of whether that rule should be reopened.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
EMPATHY IS CONTINUOUS — locked July 2026, carried forward
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Warmth never drops as the case deepens. There is no acknowledgement
cap anywhere. What increases through a case is depth, precision and
directive authority — never temperature. Manager energy is
direction, not distance.

Two bounds keep this honest: in crisis, warmth is expressed through
speed; and padding is prevented by the closure protocol, not by
capping empathy. Empathy is proportional to the moment — never
filler before a move, never performance repeated across turns.

The one-clause acknowledgement cap in the validated reference
prompt is deliberately not carried into deployment.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE CORE DIFFERENTIATOR — locked June 2026
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Most tools tell passengers what they are entitled to. Airdesk tells
them what to DO and what to ASK FOR — the next right move, revealed
one at a time. Not a script. Not a lecture. A wingman flies
alongside; the passenger stays in control.

The moat is insider operational knowledge from seven years across
airline operations (three years) and online travel agency work
(four years). What a generic AI or a public regulation guide cannot
replicate: waiver code mechanics on the agency side; involuntary
endorsement checks; the extraordinary-circumstances challenge and
its written-evidence demand; alliance inventory unlock; the online
travel agency blame-loop break; and goodwill-gesture and
settlement-form traps. All delivered as direction, never as
verbatim script.

WHERE THE PRODUCT STANDS — locked 13 August 2026. Airdesk reasons
from three seats at once: the airline agent at the desk, the travel
agency agent servicing the booking, and the passenger. The first
two are knowledge. The third is position. The product stands in the
passenger's seat and advocates from there — that is not a
credential line, it is where the product operates from, and it is
what makes it the passenger's side of the desk rather than a
neutral explainer. The prompt currently expresses the first two
only; correcting that is queued in What Is Pending.

MINIMUM IDENTITY MODEL — locked 13 August 2026. Airdesk works from
the situation, not the person. One identifier only, held solely as
a return address so a passenger can come back to their case, and
nothing else about the person ever. No name, no passport, no
booking reference, no ticket number, no card data held by us.

SUPERSEDES the zero-personal-data model locked June 2026. That
claim was factually false once payment captured an email: an email
address is personal data in every target jurisdiction, and using it
to identify someone is processing it. The substance the founder
intended is unchanged and is now stated accurately, which matters
because this sentence ends up in a privacy policy, where a false
claim is a liability rather than a wording preference.

TEST PLATFORM — locked 13 August 2026
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Product testing runs on the Test Bench, a separate Claude project
holding the prompt under test as its instructions plus the
knowledge base as its sole knowledge file. Nothing else — never the
test file itself.

Founder's reasoning: an active AI tool will always be part of the
shipped product, so testing on a frontier model tests the real
thing rather than a proxy.

This closes a standing contradiction. A self-hosted stack —
open-weight models on the founder's own hardware — was explored in
July on data-sovereignty grounds and is not adopted. Two findings
from that exploration are retained: paid model APIs contractually
do not train on API traffic by default, so the concern that drove
the exploration was partly inaccurate; and self-hosting retains
independent commercial logic, because business-layer buyers
handling passenger personal data may demand it. That is a licensing
argument, not a testing one.

Scenarios are authored in the build project, executed on the Test
Bench in fresh chats, and graded back in the build project against
the answer key. A pasted product response is graded against the
knowledge base and the test file — never by regenerating the answer
first. When the prompt or knowledge base version changes, the Test
Bench updates before any run. The weakest available model is banned
from all test runs. A run under the wrong configuration is void,
not failed.

PRODUCT SCOPE — FULL TRIP LIFECYCLE — locked 13 August 2026
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Airdesk is present across the whole trip, not only when it breaks.
A guiding angel rather than a complaints desk. Four phases, all in
scope:

  Pre-travel guidance — what to know before you fly.
  In-trip companionship — present while travelling.
  Disruption advisory — the core, when something goes wrong.
  Post-disruption support — claims, drafts, follow-through.

DELIBERATELY NICHE. Founder position: the product is not built for
everyone, and a narrower market with real depth is preferred to a
broad one without it.

WHY IT IS NOT OPTIONAL: a subscription needs a reason to be held
while nothing is broken. Serious disruption is rare — most
subscribers will go a full term without one. Without the rest of
the trip, the model asks people to pay for something they may never
use and then renew. Companionship is what gives the subscription
weekly texture rather than once-every-few-years texture.

THE HONEST COUNTER, recorded so it is not lost: general travel
advice is not defensible. Any capable model answers a budget or
destination question. The moat is disruption knowledge, and
widening toward generic content dilutes it. The resolution is that
companionship must be built from the same insider expertise pointed
forwards — which airlines rebook well when things break, which
connections are genuinely safe at which hubs, what a fare rule
means before you buy it, what to do in the first ten minutes when a
flight looks shaky. Not a general travel assistant.

SEQUENCED AFTER FIRST PROOF. Locked as scope, built later. Every
milestone criterion runs through documented outcomes, and outcomes
come from disruption — a well-planned trip generates no outcome and
no proof. The smallest real slice stays disruption-only so that a
paying stranger produces a documented result as soon as possible.
Companionship follows.

CONSEQUENCES QUEUED, not yet executed: the prompt defines the role
as "the person someone talks to when a trip goes wrong," which
excludes three of the four phases; the five data points are
disruption-shaped and have no meaning in a pre-travel conversation;
elicitation must recognise which phase it is in before it knows
what to ask, which interacts directly with state-adaptive bundling;
and the knowledge base holds no pre-travel content at all. All in
What Is Pending.

INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY — locked August 2026
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

COPYRIGHT. The knowledge base is protectable as an original
compilation — the selection, arrangement and expression, not the
underlying regulations, which are public facts. The prompt files
are protectable as written works. Protection is automatic on
creation; no registration is required in most target markets. The
Contributions Audit is the evidence of authorship, which is why it
stays a discrete file permanently. Keep it current.

PATENTS — ASSESSED AND REJECTED, August 2026. An advisory workflow
built on public regulations is unlikely to clear novelty and
subject-matter requirements in the United Kingdom or Europe, would
be slow and expensive in the United States, and would require
publishing the method. Copyright over the compilation, plus the
outcome record, is the real protection. Revisit only if a genuinely
novel technical mechanism is invented.

TRADEMARK — NOT DONE. The product name has not been cleared. A
search in the primary markets is required before any spending on
branding and before the name appears on anything public.

LICENSING. Business-layer contracts need evergreen licence terms
and change-of-control provisions, so that an acquirer cannot
devalue the asset by terminating licences. Not drafted.

The Advisory Product Shell is retained by the founder on any exit
of the aviation product. This is the position that survives every

CASE RECORDING PROTOCOL — locked June 2026
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

From day one of the Deployable Product, every conversation is
noted. Not trained on. Not integrated into the knowledge base. Not
paired to outcomes yet. Just recorded.

Eight fields captured automatically from conversation data: date;
departure airport; airline; disruption type; regime applied;
compensation tier where applicable; booking channel; outcome
status.

Two status levels. RECORD means the case was received and the
outcome is not yet known. DOCUMENTED OUTCOME means the user
confirmed a result in writing — refund, compensation, rebooking, or
denied-boarding payment — with permission to reference it.

Records upgrade to documented outcomes when the user returns and
confirms. This is the evidence pipeline: ten documented outcomes is
a Demonstrated for Subscriptions criterion; fifty across three or
more jurisdictions is a Deal-Ready criterion.

These records are the evidence database. They are the raw material
for the business-layer conversation.
They are never shown to users. Anonymised aggregate data informs
the business-layer pitch.

CASES NEVER WRITE DIRECTLY TO THE KNOWLEDGE BASE. Founder-gated
canonization only, with anecdotal basis tags.

THE FIRING LOGIC — six stages, domain-agnostic:
1. UNKNOWN RIGHT. The user has a right, entitlement or value they
   do not know they have. The entry condition.
2. QUANTIFICATION. Convert the unknown right to a specific number
   or estimate. A vague entitlement is easy to defer; a specific
   figure is not.
3. NAMED GAP. Name precisely why the user cannot collect it alone.
   Not "it's complicated" — the specific institutional friction.
4. PROOF OF ACCESS. One piece of insider knowledge that proves the
   gap can be closed. Not everything — one thing.
5. ANCHOR MOMENT — SUPERSEDED August 2026. This stage existed to
   place a paywall inside a conversation. Payment now happens
   before the conversation begins, so the stage has no function in
   the aviation instance. Whether it survives as a domain-agnostic
   stage for other domains is an open question; see What Is
   Pending.
6. OUTPUT. The artifact — a document, a claim letter, a script —
   something that exists outside the product and goes to work in
   the world. This is also the word-of-mouth unit.

THE TWO-PERSONA ARCHITECTURE within the Shell — SUPERSEDED August
2026. The Shell still encodes a free intake layer and a paid
advisory layer with prompt templates for both. This contradicts the
